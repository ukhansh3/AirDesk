# Sunshine + Moonlight proof of concept

1. Record installed versions, license and distribution terms.
2. Start host and verify standalone Moonlight client screen reception.
3. Verify pointer and keyboard semantics without bypassing PLEOS driving restrictions.
4. Distinguish standalone app from embedded Android module.
5. Measure latency/fps/reconnect and PLEOS app area letterbox.
6. Ensure unsafe safety event blocks view/input locally.
No third-party binaries are committed until approval.
